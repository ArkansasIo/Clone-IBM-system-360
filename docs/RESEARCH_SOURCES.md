# Research sources

Use primary manuals and record the exact publication/model for every behavior implemented.

- IBM 1401 Reference Manual (public archival scan): https://bitsavers.org/pdf/ibm/1401/A24-1403-5_1401_Reference_Apr62.pdf
- IBM 1401 and 729 historical information: https://ibm1401.computerhistory.org/729-Info.html
- IBM System/360 Principles of Operation (archival scan): https://www.eserviceinfo.com/preview_html.php?fileid=79665&previewid=50152
- Bitsavers IBM manuals archive: https://bitsavers.org/pdf/ibm/

## Evidence policy
Store a manual citation, page/section, a short description, and a regression test alongside nontrivial architectural behavior. Archive sources may be scanned and model-specific; do not treat secondary summaries as authoritative for instruction semantics.
