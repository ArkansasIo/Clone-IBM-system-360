# Research notes

The IBM 1401 was introduced in 1959 and used character-oriented decimal processing. IBM 729 was a family of tape drives with model-dependent densities and speeds. System/360 was announced in 1964 as a compatible family using an 8-bit byte architecture; 2400-series drives were used for nine-track tape.

References:
- IBM 1401 reference manual: https://bitsavers.org/pdf/ibm/1401/A24-1403-5_1401_Reference_Apr62.pdf
- IBM 729 notes: https://ibm1401.computerhistory.org/729-Info.html
- System/360 Principles of Operation: https://www.eserviceinfo.com/preview_html.php?fileid=79665&previewid=50152

Model-specific manuals are required to validate exact CPU/device behavior. This repository contains independently written code, not original IBM source listings.
